## 14. GoWild! Pass™

Enrollment in the Frontier GoWild! Pass™ Club (“GoWild! Pass”) is conditioned on acceptance by you of the terms and conditions contained in these Rules. By purchasing the GoWild! Pass or using or continuing to use the benefits of GoWild! Pass, the holder is deemed to have agreed to all such terms and conditions.

### GoWild! Pass™ Eligibility

- The purchaser must be 18 years or older and a resident of the United States to enroll themselves or another individual in the GoWild! Pass™. The GoWild! Pass holder must be a resident of the United States and a Member of the FRONTIER Miles frequent flier program. If the pass holder is not already a member of FRONTIER Miles when enrolling in GoWild! Pass, the pass holder will automatically be enrolled in FRONTIER Miles.

- The purchaser and the pass holder must provide and maintain accurate and true personal information when applying for a GoWild! Pass™ or using any GoWild! Pass benefits. Failure to do so may invalidate the GoWild! Pass and any subsequent fare purchase, at Frontier's sole discretion. It is the pass holder’s responsibility to update personal information within the FRONTIER Miles Account Profile through the Account Profile page https://www.flyfrontier.com/myfrontier/.

- To access the Account Profile page, go to the https://www.flyfrontier.com/ home page, click on the “account sign in” link, and log in to the account. Then click on the name on the upper right-hand of the page and click on “My Profile.”

- The GoWild! Pass™ may be canceled at any time by selecting the cancel option within the FRONTIER Miles account profile on the Site. Go to flyfrontier.com, click on the “account sign in” link, and log in to the account. Then click on the name on the upper right hand of the page and click on “My Profile.” GoWild! Pass™ status and ability to cancel are on this page.

- The GoWild! Pass™ is non-transferable. Only the pass holder may use the benefits of the GoWild! Pass™. Promptly notify Frontier upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use of the GoWild! Pass™.

### GoWild! Pass™ Usage

- The GoWild! Pass gives the pass holder the ability to book a ticket at an airfare of $0.01 on select flights operated by Frontier Airlines. The GoWild! Pass does not guarantee the availability of air transportation services.

- The ability to make GoWild! Pass bookings is based on flight and seat availability; not all Frontier flights will be available for booking through the GoWild! Pass. Last seat availability is not guaranteed.

- All taxes, fees, and charges remain the responsibility of the GoWild! Pass holder and must be paid at the time of booking.

- GoWild! Pass tickets do not include advance seat assignments, carry-on baggage, checked baggage or other ancillary products unless otherwise stated. Ancillary products can be purchased separately.

- GoWild! Pass™ tickets become available for booking the day before flight departure for domestic travel and ten (10) days before flight departure for international travel. Bookings can be made at FlyFrontier.com or via other channels as may be specified by Frontier. Frontier may offer extended promotional booking periods to passholders at its sole discretion.  Early bookings and peak travel day bookings may incur additional airfare and/or charges. Early booking and peak travel day charges are posted at www.flyfrontier.com/optional-services.

- GoWild! Pass flights are subject to peak travel days. Peak travel days are posted at www.flyfrontier.com/deals/gowild-pass

- The GoWild! Pass holder is the only person allowed to book and to travel using the GoWild! Pass. Pass holders may not purchase tickets for resale or to artificially consume seat inventory.

- Pass holders should only book flights that they intend to board. Pass holders may not book flights that overlap each other, that are duplicate itineraries, or that are impossible to fly.

- GoWild! Pass is not compatible with other Frontier sales and promotions unless specifically advertised.

- No Shows are subject to the terms set forth in Frontier’s Contract of Carriage. GoWild! Pass holders who repeatedly book and no-show flights, or who otherwise violate the terms and conditions contained in these Rules, are subject to financial penalties up to and including the revocation of pass privileges without refund.

- Changes and/or cancellations to itineraries booked using a GoWild! pass will be processed subject to the terms and conditions of that itinerary, including, without limitation, the then current change and/or cancel fee(s) in effect plus any difference in airfare for the alternate requested date(s) or flight(s).

- All promotions, fares, and any other special offers, whether through Frontier or any of its partners/affiliates, are subject to the terms and conditions

- Air travel on Frontier (including GoWild! Pass travel) is governed by Frontier’s Contract of Carriage, a copy of which is available at www.flyfrontier.com, and at all Frontier Airlines locations. Please review the Ticket Terms and Conditions and Contract of Carriage for travel requirements and restrictions, liability and baggage limitations, and requirements for the travel of minor children. Visit our baggage information page for additional requirements and restrictions. Frontier’s Customer Service Plan and Contingency Plan for Lengthy Tarmac Delays also apply to flights operated by Frontier. For clarity, these provisions only apply to the extent applicable for flights operated by Frontier.

- Passholders are responsible for complying with all international travel and immigration requirements including, but not limited to, providing evidence of confirmed return or onward travel where applicable.

- Children age of 14 years or younger must travel accompanied by a passenger who is at least 15 years old. A birth certificate, official school ID, or other form of ID may be requested for age verification purposes if the child's age appears questionable. Passengers under age 18 traveling without both parents may need additional documentation to travel across international borders, depending on the country's requirements.

### Special Renewal Terms for Passes Expiring April 30, 2027

The following provisions apply only to eligible holders of a GoWild! Annual Pass scheduled to expire on April 30, 2027.

- Frontier may offer an eligible Pass holder a one-time opportunity to renew before the scheduled renewal date for $499 (the “Early-Renewal Price”). Frontier determines eligibility and will display or communicate the offer to the eligible Pass holder. The offer is personal to the eligible Pass holder and may not be sold, assigned, transferred, or combined with another Pass-purchase or renewal offer unless Frontier expressly permits otherwise.

- Complete early renewal for $499. To receive the extended travel period at the $499 Early-Renewal Price, an eligible Pass holder must sign in to their FRONTIER Miles account, access the early-renewal offer available to them, and follow the offer instructions to complete the $499 purchase. Early renewal is complete only when Frontier successfully processes the payment and issues a renewal confirmation. The current Pass will remain valid through April 30, 2027, and the renewed Pass travel period will run from May 1, 2027, through December 31, 2028. The renewal extends the named Pass holder’s existing Pass; it does not create a second or transferable Pass.

- If the Pass holder does not renew early. If the Pass holder does not complete the $499 early-renewal purchase, the Pass is scheduled to renew automatically on April 30, 2027, for $599 (the “Standard Renewal Price”), unless the Pass holder cancels in accordance with the applicable cancellation requirements before the renewal charge is processed. Taking no action does not cancel automatic renewal. After successful processing of the $599 payment, the renewed Pass travel period will also run from May 1, 2027, through December 31, 2028. Both renewal options therefore provide the same renewed travel period; completing the early-renewal offer secures that period at $499 rather than $599.

- The applicable Renewal Price will be charged to the payment method authorized under the GoWild! Pass Terms and Conditions. Cancellation requirements, payment processing and failed-payment treatment, and refund eligibility are governed by those terms and any renewal disclosures presented to and accepted by the Pass holder.

- Renewal does not guarantee transportation; flight, seat, or last-seat availability; a particular route or destination; or a particular fare. All travel is subject to applicable booking windows, inventory availability, fares, Peak Day treatment, taxes, fees, charges, Frontier's Contract of Carriage, and the GoWild! Pass Terms and Conditions in effect on the applicable travel date.
