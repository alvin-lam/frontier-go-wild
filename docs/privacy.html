The short version

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. App Name has no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. We never receive, see or store what you enter.

What is saved on your device

App Name saves the following in your browser's local storage so it is still there next time:

  • Your light, dark or automatic mode, and your color choice.
  • When you closed the Add to Home Screen message.

Installing the app also keeps a copy of the site on your device so it works offline. Clearing your browser's site data or uninstalling the app removes everything, and we cannot recover it.

What your device tells App Name

App Name reads your device's time zone and language setting to format dates, numbers and units and to choose season and holiday colors. These settings are used only on your device and never leave it.

Where the site is served from

Every file the page uses, including its fonts and data, comes from this site. The page makes no requests to other services. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Cloudflare provides its domain name service. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and basic browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. Neither receives what you enter.

Children

App Name does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If App Name ever starts collecting information, such as anonymous visit counts, this page will be updated first to explain what is collected and why. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to report[at]alltripsconsidered[dot]com.
