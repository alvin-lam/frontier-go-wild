Frontier Airlines Go Wild! ✈️

A plain-language guide to the GoWild! Pass rules: who can enroll, when to book, what each pass costs and which days are peak days.

How the GoWild! Pass works

The pass lets the pass holder book select Frontier flights at a base fare of $0.01. Seats are limited, and not every flight is offered.

Who can enroll

  • The buyer must be at least 18 and live in the United States. The buyer can enroll themselves or someone else.
  • The pass holder must live in the United States and be a FRONTIER Miles member. Anyone who is not yet a member is enrolled automatically.
  • The pass is not transferable. Only the pass holder can book with it and fly on it.

When you can book

  • Domestic flights: from the day before departure.
  • International flights: from 10 days before departure.
  • Booking earlier, or flying on a peak travel day, can cost more. Frontier posts these charges on its optional services page.

What the fare covers

  • The pass holder pays all taxes, fees and charges when booking.
  • Seat selection, carry-on bags and checked bags are not included. They can be bought separately.
  • The pass cannot be combined with other Frontier sales or promotions unless an offer says so.

Booking rules

  • Book only flights you plan to take. Overlapping, duplicate or impossible itineraries are not allowed, and neither is booking for resale.
  • Changes and cancellations follow the ticket's normal rules, including current change or cancel fees plus any fare difference.
  • Repeated no-shows or other rule breaking can lead to penalties, up to losing the pass with no refund.
  • Children 14 or younger must fly with someone at least 15 years old. Minors crossing borders without both parents may need extra documents.
  • On international trips, you must meet entry rules, which can include proof of a return or onward flight.

Payment, renewal and canceling

  • The first payment is not refundable. The card used becomes the default card in the account's Wallet.
  • Passes renew automatically. Renewal charges are not refundable. Frontier may charge another saved card if the default card fails.
  • To stop renewal, cancel in your FRONTIER Miles profile before midnight Mountain Time on the last day of the current term. For the 2027 Annual Pass, the deadline is October 31, 2027.
  • Frontier may change the pass terms at any time without notice. It must give notice before changing the renewal price.
